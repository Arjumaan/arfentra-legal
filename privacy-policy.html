    Privacy Policy

    How Arfentra Private Limited handles personal data across its website, applications and client services.

    Effective date: 15 November 2026 Last updated: 15 November 2026 Version 1.0

    1. Introduction and scope

    Arfentra Private Limited (“Arfentra”, “we”, “us”, “our”) is a company incorporated in India (CIN U62013TZ2026PTC041310) that designs and delivers AI automations, agentic workflows, web applications, mobile applications, enterprise software and related IT services, including AI, cybersecurity, cloud computing and blockchain services (the “Services”), mainly for businesses in logistics, hospitality, restaurants and other industries.

    This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share and protect personal data when you visit our website (currently sentrasec.in, and arfentra.com once it is live), contact us, sign in to our applications, engage us for a project, or use software we build or operate. It applies to our website, web and mobile applications, social media pages, proposals and client engagements.

    Our separate products, CT – Upskilling and Book Your Food, have their own privacy policies.

    This Policy is issued under the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the rules made under it, as in force from time to time. By using our website or Services you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use them.

    2. Our role: Data Fiduciary and Data Processor

    • As a Data Fiduciary: for data about website visitors, enquirers, prospects, account holders and our clients’ authorised representatives, we decide why and how the data is processed.
    • As a Data Processor: when we build, host or operate a system for a client and process that client’s customer, employee or operational data for it, the client is the Data Fiduciary. We process that data only on the client’s documented instructions. The client is responsible for having a lawful basis and any required consents, and its own privacy notice also applies.

    3. Information we collect

    Information you give us

    Name, designation, company name, business email address, phone number, billing details, GSTIN (where applicable), project requirements, messages, feedback and any files you share with us.

    Information from your Google Account

    If you choose Sign in with Google on one of our applications, we receive your name, email address and profile picture, and the unique identifier Google assigns to your account. See Section 4 for details.

    Information collected automatically

    IP address, device and browser type, operating system, pages visited, referring URL, time spent, log data and cookie or similar identifiers.

    Client operational data

    Data from client systems that we handle during development, integration, testing, support or hosting, for example shipment records, bookings, guest records, orders, inventory and employee records. We access only what is needed for the project.

    Payment information

    Payments are handled by third-party payment gateways such as Razorpay or by our own payment gateway. We do not store full card numbers, UPI PINs or net-banking credentials on our servers.

    Information from public sources

    Business details from LinkedIn, Instagram, company websites or public registries, used for business communication.

    We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal data, such as health or biometric data, unless a project specifically requires it and a written agreement covers it.

    4. Google user data and Sign in with Google

    Limited Use disclosure. Arfentra’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

    What we access

    Our applications use Google OAuth for sign-in and request only these basic scopes: openid, email and profile. This gives us your name, email address, profile picture and Google account identifier. We do not read your Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Contacts or other Google content through sign-in.

    If a specific application needs additional Google permissions, Google will show them on its consent screen and ask for your approval first. We will describe them in this Policy or in that application’s own notice before requesting them.

    How we use it

    • to create and secure your account and recognise you when you return;
    • to show your name and picture inside the application;
    • to send service messages such as account, security and support notices.

    What we do not do

    • We do not sell Google user data.
    • We do not use it for advertising, including personalised or retargeted advertising.
    • We do not transfer it to others except to provide or improve the features you use, to follow the law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with your notice where required.
    • We do not allow people to read it, except with your consent, for security investigation, to comply with law, or where it is aggregated and anonymised for internal operations.
    • We do not use it to train general-purpose AI models.

    Your control

    You can withdraw Arfentra’s access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete the account data we hold, email arfentra@gmail.com from the same email address and we will delete it as set out in Section 12.

    Where we build an application for a client that uses Google sign-in, the client is the Data Fiduciary for the Google user data in that application, and the client’s privacy policy also applies.

    5. Why we use personal data

    We process personal data on the basis of your consent, performance of a contract, compliance with legal obligations, and other uses permitted by law, for these purposes:

    • responding to enquiries and preparing proposals and quotations;
    • delivering, testing, deploying, hosting, supporting and improving the Services;
    • invoicing, payment processing, accounting and tax compliance;
    • account administration, project communication, notices and support;
    • securing our systems, preventing fraud and misuse, and enforcing our terms;
    • analysing website use and improving our offerings;
    • sending updates, case studies or offers, which you can opt out of at any time;
    • complying with law, court orders and lawful requests of authorities.

    6. AI and automation processing

    Our Services include AI models and agentic workflows. We use our own proprietary AI models and infrastructure. We do not use client confidential data or personal data to train general-purpose models available to others. Where client data is used to configure, fine-tune or evaluate a model for a specific client, this is done only on the client’s written instruction, and the resulting model is restricted to that client’s deployment unless agreed otherwise in writing.

    AI outputs may contain errors. Clients should keep appropriate human oversight for decisions that significantly affect individuals.

    7. Cookies and analytics

    Our website and applications use cookies and similar technologies for essential functions such as sign-in and security, for performance analytics and for preferences. You can control cookies through your browser settings or any cookie banner we show. Disabling some cookies may affect how the website works. We do not use cookies to track children or to show them targeted advertising.

    8. Sharing and disclosure

    We do not sell personal data. We share it only as follows:

    • Service providers: cloud infrastructure (Google Cloud Platform, Microsoft Azure and Amazon Web Services, depending on the project), payment gateways (Razorpay and our own gateway), and communication, analytics and project-management tools. They process data on our instructions and under confidentiality obligations.
    • Clients and project partners: as needed to deliver a project.
    • Professional advisers: lawyers, auditors, accountants and insurers, under confidentiality.
    • Legal and regulatory: where required by law, court order or government authority, or to protect our rights, users or the public.
    • Business transfers: in a merger, acquisition or restructuring, with this Policy continuing to protect the data.

    9. Data storage and transfers

    At present we store and process personal data within India. If we transfer data outside India in future, we will do so only as permitted by Indian law and will update this Policy. Data is hosted on Google Cloud Platform, Microsoft Azure or Amazon Web Services infrastructure selected according to project requirements.

    10. Data security

    We apply reasonable security practices, including access controls, encryption in transit and where appropriate at rest, role-based permissions, logging, secure development practices, periodic reviews and confidentiality undertakings from staff. No system is completely secure. If a personal data breach occurs, we will notify affected persons and the authorities as required by law.

    11. How long we keep data

    We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above and to meet legal, tax, accounting and contractual requirements.

    • Enquiry data is kept for up to 24 months after the last interaction.
    • Account data, including data received from Google sign-in, is kept while your account is active and deleted or anonymised within 90 days after you close it or ask us to delete it, unless we must keep it for legal, tax, dispute or security reasons.
    • Contract and invoice records are kept as long as law requires, currently at least 8 years for accounting records.
    • Client project data is returned or deleted at the end of the engagement or support period as agreed in the contract.

    12. Your rights

    Subject to applicable law, you may:

    • access a summary of the personal data we process and who we share it with;
    • request correction, completion or updating of your data;
    • request erasure of data that is no longer needed or where you withdraw consent;
    • withdraw consent at any time, which does not affect earlier lawful processing;
    • nominate another person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity;
    • raise a grievance and, if it is not resolved, complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

    Where we act as a Data Processor, please send requests to the relevant client, and we will assist them. To exercise these rights with us, email arfentra@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity first. We aim to acknowledge requests within 48 hours and resolve them within 30 days.

    13. Children

    Our Services are directed at businesses and are not intended for persons under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data of children through our website or applications. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it. Where a client’s system that we build processes children’s data, the client is responsible for verifiable parental consent as required by law.
